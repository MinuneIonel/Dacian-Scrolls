# WP-20 handoff: combat AI and balance

- `Ai.choose(battle, uid, rng)` by enemy `ai` (brute, skirmisher, caster, boss_surgeon); party-side AI units (summons, charmed) fight as brutes. `Ai.policy_good` / `Ai.policy_random` drive party units for sims and the bot. Each call returns one member of `legal_actions(uid)` (or `{}` if that list is empty); call again until the turn changes.
- Scoring: expected damage x hit chance + kill bonus + style target preference; moves scored by attack reach with AP left, BFS distance to an attack cell, cover, adjacency threats. Every chosen action spends AP; a per-turn cap (16, random 6) ends turns.
- Boss: round 1 places gravity zone + specimen tank (once per kind), pushes into gravity/acid, summons once at <= 50% HP (spawn-summon ability, not by id), else attacks the most dangerous (best stat + weapon).
- `tools/balance.sh <root> <enc|all> <n> <good|random|both> [--party= --level= --items= --seed=]`; results and the data/ tuning section in `docs/balance.md`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_ai.gd` (21). Fixtures `fxa_*` + `strings/ai.csv`.

Contract notes (readings I chose; please confirm or amend CONTRACTS):
- Hit roll is one d20, rolling high: hit when `d20 * 5 > 100 - chance` (95% hits on 2+), natural 20 always hits and crits. Same shape as the section 7 roll formula.
- `legal_actions(uid)` returns [] for any uid other than `current_unit()` and after the battle ends. Flee also needs a non-summoned, non-charmed party unit; a failed flee ends the turn.
- Statuses tick down at the end of the holder's own turn. Bleeding/regen/acid (3) apply at turn start, after `turn_started`. Charmed flips the unit's effective side and makes it AI-driven; "win" still counts original enemies.
- Enemies and summons don't pay mana/charge (only party characters do). Gear `mods` add `damage` (weapon_damage), `armor` and stat keys.
- Summons: party-side summons get `hp = max_hp / 2` (max_hp = def hp); enemy-side `spawn` summons get full HP. `from_corpse` is true only for `from: corpse`. Summons leave no corpse and drop no loot. Gibbed enemies still leave a corpse (rule read literally).
- Downed party units are not targetable and do not block. `died` is emitted for downed party units too (gib/corpse false). party_hp shows 1 for downed only on a win; on lose/flee they stay at 0.
- Change request (additive): `result()` also has `items_used: {item: n}`; `ability_used` from an item also has `item`. `hazard` with `turns: 0` means a hazard expired. Hazards tick down at each new round.

# WP-13 handoff: world travel, fog, encounters, UFOs

- `Travel.path(state, to, defs)`: 4-way Dijkstra by terrain hours; `[]` when unreachable, impassable, outside or already there (no time passes). `Travel.step(state, next, rng, defs)` only accepts a passable 4-neighbour (else `[]`, no change). Helpers: `passable`, `hours_at`, `hides`, `world_size`, `advance_time(state, hours)`.
- Step event order: `stepped`, (`food_changed`, `starved`) per midnight, `time_changed`, `revealed`, `discovered`, `arrived`, `encounter`, then Ufo events (`flag_set` for decoy, `ufo_moved`, `abducted`).
- Starving: food to 0, members above 1 HP lose 2 (floor 1), `starved{amount: 2}`. Encounter or abduction sets `flags["enc_cooldown"] = 3`; each later step decrements it instead of rolling. No roll on any location cell.
- Hidden locations are never discovered by walking; `arrived` fires only for discovered locations (hidden ones need `["discover"]`).
- UFO patrol: 1 cell (8-way) toward `route[route_i]`, advancing on arrival, looping; off-route UFOs fly back the same way. Distances are Chebyshev. Detection switches to chase and moves 2 cells in the same tick. Abducted only while chasing, at most one per tick. `decoy_charm` is checked only on patrol->chase and emits `flag_set`.
- `Encounters.options` -> `{id, label_key, chance}`; `choose` returns `[]` for a hidden/unknown option; roll uses one `rng.roll(20)` and emits `roll`. XP when the result has no `battle_requested` and the option is not flee.
- `apply_battle_result`: HP/mana/charge clamped to max; downed back at 1 HP on win AND on fled (keeps the world-map HP >= 1 invariant); loot (sorted ids) and XP only on win. The UFO with this encounter on the party cell becomes `gone` on a win, `patrol` otherwise (`ufo_moved`).
- Tests: `test_travel.gd` (incl. 300-journey fuzz), `test_fog.gd`, `test_ufo.gd`, `test_encounters.gd`. Fixtures: `encounters/fxw_bandits.json`, `strings/world.csv`.
- No contract change requests. Gap: after a lost/fled UFO battle the UFO may re-detect at once (WP-30 glue decides).

# WP-15 Audio pipeline and the Sarmizegetusa track
- **Regenerate:** `python3 tools/audio/build.py` (~1 min, deterministic, byte-stable; `build.py sfx gib` for one group/id). Then `python3 tools/audio/test_audio.py` (ids, equal stem samples, peaks <= -1 dBFS, seams, size) and `python3 tools/audio/preview.py` (spectrogram PNGs + RMS + seam report in build/audio_preview/).
- Kit: `tools/audio/synth.py` (nai, cobza Karplus-Strong, drone, dairea, toba, tulnic, polyBLEP saw pad, arp, kick/snare/hat, circular reverb/delay/STFT sweep filter, master). Songs in `music.py`, one-shots in `sfx.py`.
- `sarmizegetusa`: 96 BPM, 32 bars (80.0 s), D Dorian #4, form A A' B A''. Stems explore / combat / lobizi. `saucer`: 16 bars (40.0 s), stems lobizi / combat, detuned tulnic duo on harmonics 5 and 7 of D.
- Stingers: abduction page_found level_up defeat. SFX: step hit miss crit gib heal summon ui_click ui_back coin door beam alarm. 4.5 MB total, OGG q4.
- `Audio.play_music(track)`: starts all stems in one AudioStreamSynchronized, base layer on (explore, or lobizi in the saucer), others off, 1.5 s crossfade from the old track. Same track again: nothing happens. Unknown track: ignored.
- `Audio.set_layer(layer, on, fade_s)`: fades by per-stream volume; layers the current track lacks (and any call with no music playing) do nothing. Switching track resets layers.
- `Audio.sfx(id)` (8-player pool, small pitch jitter on step/hit), `Audio.stinger(id)` (ducks the stems 8 dB), `Audio.stop_music(fade_s)`. Unknown ids are silent, no errors.
- Buses made at runtime: Stems -> Music -> Master, SFX -> Master. Music/SFX bus volumes follow `Settings.music_volume/sfx_volume` on `Settings.changed` (0 mutes).
- Extra queries: `current_track`, `is_playing()`, `has_layer()`, `layer_level()`, `music_player()`, `stream_for(kind, id)`.
- Loading: imported resource via load(), else `AudioStreamOggVorbis.load_from_file()`. `.ogg.import` files are committed (loop is set in code).
- Gap/request: CI does not run `tools/audio/test_audio.py` (needs ffmpeg); the orchestrator may add it to ci.yml. Nobody has listened to the music yet; checked only by spectrogram, RMS and seam.
- Gotcha: stopping a playing stream and quitting a few headless frames later leaks playbacks at exit; `test_audio.gd` waits 0.3 s in after_all.

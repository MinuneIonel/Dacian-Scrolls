# WP-32 handoff: gore and juice
- `src/modes/combat/fx/combat_fx.gd` (node `CombatFx`, `combat.fx`): `setup(combat_view)` connects to the EventPlayer's `unit_damaged`, `unit_died`, `event_started`; draws only, own RandomNumberGenerator, never touches Battle/Game.rng/events. Reads `Settings.gore` once in setup (field `gore`).
- Deaths: gore 0 dust puff (the player's fade stays); 1 splat decal as the body falls; 2, or `gib` at >= 1: body hides, 6 chunks (`gib.gd`) arc and stay, splats, shake. 2x2 at gore 2: `fx_balloon_burst` x1.6, splats on every walkable cell of the 4x4 around it, 8 chunks, big shake. Machines: black oil and grey scrap. Downed party: dust or a small splat, never chunks. Raisable corpses keep their node (sprite hidden) so they stay targetable.
- Juice: 60 ms hit pause on crits (`Engine.time_scale = 0`, real-time timer, restored in `_exit_tree`); camera shake/nudge via `camera.offset` (pushes nudge 4 px along the push); hit puffs; units bob 3 px while gravity is `floaty` or on a `gravity_zone` cell. Heal sparkle on `healed`, magic glyph / tech zap on `ability_used` by source. Damage numbers were already WP-21's `_float`.
- Decals live in `map_root/Decals` (z -8: over ground, under overlay and units). Groups: `fx_splat fx_gib fx_decal fx_puff fx_anim`; `fx.counts` tallies spawns; `shakes pauses nudges` counters.
- `src/ui/fx/puff.gd` (code-drawn puff), `src/ui/fx/one_shot.gd` (plays a `kind: fx` frames entry once, honours its anchor) are reusable by any view.
- Hookup (only edit outside owned files): `combat.gd` +7 lines: `const CombatFx := preload(...)`, `var fx: Node`, and 4 lines at the end of `_build_player()` creating/adding/setup of CombatFx. `event_player.gd` untouched. Props-anchor gap left alone (does not affect fx).
- Shots: `tools/shots.sh res://src/modes/combat/fx/fx_preview.tscn out.png 98 1280x720 --args='{"gore":2,"encounter_id":"bancul_patrol","gib":false}'` (debug party, no turn loop, fake deaths for every enemy, view only).
- Tests `tests/integration/test_fx.gd` (8): seeded fx_wolves and fx_saucer battles give identical Bus event lists at gore 0 and 2 (and chunks only at 2); per-level node counts (0/1/1+gib/2 incl. balloon), hit pause restores time, push nudge, ability fx, floaty bob on/off.
- Gaps: `fx_tractor_beam` unused (no event fits; WP-30/world map could use one_shot for abductions). Enemies standing right behind a front wall/tall tile hide their splat too (map art). No sound for splats beyond existing hit/crit sfx.

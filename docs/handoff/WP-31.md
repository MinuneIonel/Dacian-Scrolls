# WP-31 handoff: the bot and the fuzzers
- `tools/test.sh tests/bot` (~35 s, real data/): 26 tests. `bot.gd` plays through the core like the views do: Travel.step loop (abducted > encounter), Encounters, location containers/triggers/talks (location.gd rules), Battle with Ai.policy_good vs Ai.choose (combat.gd loop), then `Encounters.apply_battle_result` + the pure `Flow.begin_abduction/after_battle` (called directly, not replicated). Checks HP >= 1, food >= 0, passable cell after every step; breaches go to `bot.problems`.
- `golden_path.gd`: seed 101 route (town, cave, Mucapor, fast road + loiter until abducted, saucer, crash site, Tsinna, south road for the trader/deserter pages, home). Each phase loops after a temple/forest wake-up; runs cached per process. Necromancer fights by choice; pacifist Knight never picks `fight`.
- `test_golden_path.gd` (8, ~7 s) / `test_golden_path_knight.gd` (5, ~8 s): ending state, 12 pages, both quests 100, Vezina, saucer down, Knight level within 1 of the Necromancer (both L4 now).
- `test_fuzz_travel.gd`: 500 journeys (50 games), random options, random battle results (every 25th a real Ai battle), save round trip each journey (~13 s). `test_fuzz_dialogue.gd`: 50 random walks per dialogue (cap 200) + a 600-walk coverage test that every node is reachable. `test_smoke_scenes.gd`: menu (x2), 3 locations, worldmap, combat (wolves, saucer), scroll, 60 frames each.
- Saucer: real battles only. A hurt party heals at Vezina before the road east and walks under the trees (`Bot.safe_path`, UFO-watched open cells cost more) while hurt. After 3 real losses a synthetic win is applied as a diagnostic only; it fails `test_saucer_won_in_real_battle` and the Knight test. Seed 101: Necromancer loses attempt 1 at full HP (sim 75%), wins attempt 2; Knight wins attempt 1.
- Design note: the only out-of-battle heal is Vezina's `heal_party` in town; herbs are battle-only; the forest wake (1 HP) can be next to the UFO loop with 6 grace ticks, so a careless player is re-abducted at 1 HP. The cover walk home took the Necromancer 7 days.
- Debug: the golden tests print the full route log (day, level, gold, food, pages per line) and stats.
- No contract change requests. Gaps: the bot does not equip gear or shop; the cave triggers are always fired (assumed on the walk to Duras).

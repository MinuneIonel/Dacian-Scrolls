# WP-23 handoff: world map view
- `src/modes/worldmap/worldmap.gd` (root Control) + `map_canvas.gd` (terrain on revealed cells only / route + markers overlay), `fog_layer.gd` (dithered soft edge, `alpha_at(cell)`), `ufo_shadow.gd`. The tscn is root + script.
- Art: `python3 tools/art/worldmap/gen_worldmap.py` paints `assets/worldmap/worldmap_terrain.png` (768x512 parchment map from data/world.json; rerun when terrain changes), `marker_town/cave/crash`, `worldmap_party` (Draco banner) and `manifest_worldmap.json`, which the view reads directly. Palette-only; not covered by tools/art/test_art.py (ART_DIRS lacks assets/worldmap).
- Flow: click previews `Travel.path` (dotted route, tooltip hours + food = midnights x party size), same cell again travels one `Travel.step` per `step_time` (0.15 s). Every batch goes to `Bus.emit_events`. Stops on abducted > encounter > arrived > discovered. Clicking the party's own cell on a location offers "Enter <name>?".
- Encounter panel: intro, `Encounters.options` (roll options show the % chance), `choose_option(id)`; `battle_requested` -> `Router.go(&"combat", {encounter_id, return_mode: &"worldmap", return_args: {}})`, otherwise an outcome panel (roll, narrator lines, XP) and then `ShopScreen.show_over` on `shop_requested`.
- Abduction hook: `play_abduction(ufo_id, encounter)` plays the `abduction` stinger, then goes to combat. WP-30 puts the beam cutscene there.
- UFOs are always drawn as shadows (fainter and rimless over fog, so they are a warning, not a spoiler); chasing ones pulse; lobizi music layer is on while any UFO chases.
- HUD: day/hour (+Night), food, gold, pages n/total(page defs), Party (`PartyScreen.show_over`), Scroll (`Router.go(&"scroll")`); night tint on the map only.
- Camera: left-drag, right/middle-drag, WASD/arrows, screen edges (only after the mouse has moved in the window); follows the party when it nears the edge.
- Debug args: `debug_party`, `debug_reveal_all`, `debug_encounter: id`, `debug_preview: [x, y]`.
- Tests: `tests/integration/test_worldmap.gd` (11). Strings: `data/strings/view_world.csv` (`wm_*`).
- Gaps: no hover highlight; fog rebuild is GDScript per step (~24k texels, fine on desktop); the road on the painted map leads to the hidden crash site (it is in world.json's terrain).
- No contract change requests. Suggest the orchestrator adds `assets/worldmap` to ART_DIRS once WP-25 owns map art.
